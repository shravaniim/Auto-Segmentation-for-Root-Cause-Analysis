Architecture Reference — Auto-Segmentation for Root Cause Analysis

How the engine turns raw monitoring data into a ranked root-cause report

Two views of the same pipeline: the business-level flow, and the mechanics underneath each step. Prepared for presentation.

1 — High-level flow

Five segmentation techniques discover candidate segments independently; every candidate is scored the same way and merged into one ranked report.

2 — Low-level pipeline

Every technique runs the same six-step contract. The one place that needs care is the hand-off between technique-level and cross-technique ranking.

Key terms

The metrics referenced above, defined once.

PSI
Population Stability Index — how much a segment's share of the population shifted between development and monitoring.
Bad Rate
Share of defaults/events in a segment; compared development vs. monitoring.
AUC / Gini / KS
Model discrimination metrics, each compared development vs. monitoring to measure performance decay.
Calibration Drift
Change in the Actual/Expected ratio — whether predicted risk still matches observed risk.
SIS
Segment Impact Score — weighted composite of drift and performance decay for one segment.
DIS
Drift Impact Score — additive composite of population and performance drift.
Root Cause Score
Feature-level PSI × Gini decay × population share — the metric used to compare segments across techniques.
Severity Score
Each technique's own internal ranking score — comparable within that technique only, not across techniques.
