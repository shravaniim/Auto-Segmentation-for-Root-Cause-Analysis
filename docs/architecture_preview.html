Architecture Reference — Auto-Segmentation for Root Cause Analysis

How the engine turns raw monitoring data into a ranked root-cause report

Two views of the same pipeline: the business-level flow, and the mechanics underneath each step. Prepared for presentation.

1 — High-level flow

Five segmentation techniques discover candidate segments independently; every candidate is scored the same way and merged into one ranked report. Optionally (Tab 2's Trend Analysis toggle), the same pipeline repeats once per monitoring month, adding recurrence scoring, early-warning forecasting, and a Q&A assistant.

2 — Low-level pipeline

Every technique runs the same six-step contract. The one place that needs care is the hand-off between technique-level and cross-technique ranking. The lower half of this diagram shows what's added when Trend Analysis is enabled, with the exact formulas at each step.

Key terms

The metrics referenced above, defined once.

PSI
Population Stability Index — how much a segment's share of the population shifted between development and monitoring.
Bad Rate
Share of defaults/events in a segment; compared development vs. monitoring.
AUC / Gini / KS
Model discrimination metrics, each compared development vs. monitoring to measure performance decay.
Calibration Drift
Change in the Actual/Expected ratio — whether predicted risk still matches observed risk.
SIS
Segment Impact Score — weighted composite of drift and performance decay for one segment.
DIS
Drift Impact Score — additive composite of population and performance drift.
Root Cause Score
Feature-level PSI × Gini decay × population share — the metric used to compare segments across techniques.
Severity Score
Each technique's own internal ranking score — comparable within that technique only, not across techniques.
Frequency
Trend analysis: the share of monitoring months a segment ranked in its technique's worst-10 — how often it recurred.
Recency Factor
Trend analysis: how recently a segment last appeared, decaying by half every 2 months of absence — is it still an active problem?
Consistency Score
Trend analysis: the longest unbroken streak of months a segment appeared in, divided by total months — distinguishes "stayed bad continuously" from "keeps disappearing and reappearing" at the same overall frequency.
Trend Impact Score
A weighted blend of Frequency, Recency Factor and Consistency Score into one number, used to boost a recurring segment's SIS.
Early Warning Forecast
A simple straight-line projection of a segment's Root Cause Score across the months it appeared, flagging ones on track to cross an alert threshold soon — a projection, not a statistical model.
Q&A Assistant
A chat interface (Tab 2) that answers free-form questions grounded in whichever results — classic and/or trend — are currently on screen.
